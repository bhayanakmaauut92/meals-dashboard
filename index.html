🥗 Oven Baked Kaddu (Pumpkin) 🕐 5 min prep · 25 min cook
📝 Kaddu / pumpkin - sliced📝 Spices (turmeric, cumin, chili powder, salt)📝 Oil spray
Slice kaddu into wedges. Toss with spices and a light spray of oil. Bake at 200C for 20-25 min until edges are golden. Simple carb side.
💡 Babio's go-to carb. Replaces rice/pasta for variety.
🍗 Nimkish Spiced Chicken Kheema 🕐 5 min prep · 20 min cook
📝 Chicken kheema (500g)📝 Nimkish seasoning (1 pack)📝 Onions (1 medium, diced)📝 Tomato puree (2 tbsp)📝 Oil
Heat oil, saute onions golden. Add kheema, cook till brown. Add nimkish seasoning and tomato puree. Simmer 10 min. Done.
💡 Quick homecooked meal. Goes with bread, pasta, or kaddu.
🍗 Nimkish Boneless Chicken (Oven) 🕐 10 min + marination prep · 25 min cook
📝 Boneless chicken (500g)📝 Nimkish seasoning (1 pack)📝 Oil spray📝 Dahi (2 tbsp) - optional
Marinate chicken with nimkish seasoning and dahi. Oven bake at 200C for 20-25 min or air fry. Flip halfway.
💡 Can also pan fry. The nimkish + dahi combo keeps chicken soft.
🍗 Chicken & Kaddu Bowl 🕐 5 min prep · 25 min (kaddu + chicken together) cook
📝 Oven baked kaddu📝 Nimkish chicken (kheema or boneless)📝 Salad (cucumber, carrot, cabbage)📝 Dahi on side
Plate kaddu slices, top with chicken, add fresh salad on the side. Dahi on the side. No rice/pasta needed.
💡 Babio's standard meal template: protein + kaddu carb + fresh veg + dahi
🥗 Gobhi Sabzi 🕐 5 min prep · 15 min cook
📝 Gobhi (1 head, florets)📝 Oil📝 Jeera (cumin seeds)📝 Haldi, mirch, dhaniya powder, salt
Heat oil, add jeera. Add gobhi florets, spices, stir. Cover and cook on low 12-15 min. Stir occasionally.
💡 Simple Indian style. Great side with chicken.
🥣 Masoor Dal 🕐 5 min prep · 20 min cook
📝 Masoor dal (1 cup)📝 Tomato (1, diced)📝 Onion (1, diced)📝 Garlic + ginger📝 Haldi, mirch, salt📝 Ghee for tadka
Pressure cook dal with haldi. Tadka: ghee, jeera, garlic, onion, tomato. Pour over dal. Simmer 5 min.
💡 Comfort food. Pair with kaddu or have on its own.
🥣 Arhar Dal (Toor Dal) 🕐 5 min prep · 20 min cook
📝 Arhar dal (1 cup)📝 Tomato (1)📝 Green chili (1)📝 Garlic📝 Haldi, salt📝 Ghee for tadka
Cook dal with haldi, tomato, garlic in pressure cooker. Tadka with ghee, jeera, red chili, garlic. Mix and serve.
💡 Classic. Babio's rotation dal.
🍛 Chana / Kabuli Chana 🕐 10 min + overnight soak prep · 30 min cook
📝 Kabuli chana (1 cup, soaked overnight)📝 Onion (1)📝 Tomato puree (3 tbsp)📝 Garlic + ginger📝 Chana masala, haldi, mirch, salt📝 Oil
Pressure cook soaked chana. Saute onions, add ginger-garlic, tomato puree, spices. Add chana, simmer 10 min.
💡 Hearty meal. Good for lunch.
🍳 Egg Bhurji (Indian Scrambled Eggs) 🕐 2 min prep · 8 min cook
📝 Eggs (3)📝 Onion (1 small, diced)📝 Tomato (1 small, diced)📝 Green chili (1)📝 Haldi, mirch, salt📝 Oil
Saute onions + green chili. Add tomatoes. Beat eggs with spices, pour in. Scramble on low heat. Serve with bread.
💡 Quick breakfast or lunch.

📝 Boneless chicken (200g, cooked)📝 Cucumber (diced)📝 Carrot (grated)📝 Cabbage (shredded)📝 Dahi dressing or lemon
